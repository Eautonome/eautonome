Description

The Eautonome Ontology provides an OWL representation of residential water end-use monitoring. It models domestic water uses, quality levels, and greywater reuse processes to reduce water consumption in residential buildings.

Overview

The Eautonome Ontology describes physical water infrastructure and monitoring devices in a residential observation system. These components generate observations related to domestic water use and water quality.

The ontology represents the greywater recycling infrastructure, the sensor network, and the resulting observations. It supports queries over domestic water use events, sensor status, measurement locations, pipe configurations, and greywater reuse data.

Competency Questions

Queries

IDNatural language questionExample
Q1Which sensor made the observation?The physical device that generated the data. For example, <eau:obsFlowHeaterOutput20260627T034633> is made by <eau:flowmeterHeaterOutput>.
Q2What physical property is measured?For example, <s4watr:FlowRate> or <s4watr:Conductivity>.
Q3Where is the measurement point located and which asset does it measure?For example, <eau:mpBathroomHot> is located in <eau:roomBathroom> and measures <eau:pipeBathroomHot>.
Q4What is the result of the observation?For example, a flow result may be expressed in <unit:L-PER-MIN>.
Q5What is the timestamp of the observation?For example, "2026-06-27T03:46:33+02:00"^^xsd:dateTime.
Q6What is the operational status of each device?For example, <eau:flowmeterBathroomCold> is inactive.
Q7Which temporal extent and usage activity characterize an observation collection?The temporal extent is represented with sosa:phenomenonTime and OWL-Time, and the activity is linked through eau:hasConsumptionEvent.
Q8Which procedure is used to acquire the observation?ADC sensors use <eau:eautonomeObservingProcedure>; the connected shower sensor uses <eau:showerObservingProcedure>.
Q9Which sensors have recorded calibration parameters, and what are they?For example, <eau:flowmeterHeaterOutput> has a calibration slope of 0.957 and an R2 value of 0.9988.

Additional Queries

IDNatural language questionExample
Q10Which sensors are included in the deployment?The query follows sosa:deployedSystem from <eau:eautonomeDeployment> and returns 14 sensors.
Q11Which room is associated with an observation?The room is obtained from the observation's measurement point through eau:locatedIn.
Q12Which measurement points are at operational risk?Four measurement points are monitored by a sensor whose status is inactive or planned.
Q13Which observations belong to the same collection and consumption event?sosa:hasMember links observations to a collection, while eau:hasConsumptionEvent links the collection to the corresponding water use event.

SPARQL Examples

The following SPARQL queries are examples from the evaluation. All counts below were obtained over the published ontology and the two example observation datasets.

PREFIX eau: <https://w3id.org/eautonome/>
    PREFIX sosa: <http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/>
    PREFIX s4watr: <https://saref.etsi.org/saref4watr/>
    PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

Q3

SELECT ?mp ?room ?asset WHERE {
      ?mp a eau:MeasurementPoint ;
          eau:locatedIn ?room ;
          eau:measuresOnAsset ?asset .
    }
    ORDER BY ?mp

Result: 10 measurement points.

Q9

SELECT ?sensor ?slope ?r2 WHERE {
      ?sensor a ?sensorClass .
      ?sensorClass rdfs:subClassOf* sosa:Sensor .
      ?sensor eau:hasCalibrationSlope ?slope ;
              eau:hasCalibrationR2 ?r2 .
    }
    ORDER BY ?sensor

Result: 6 sensors with recorded calibration slope and R2.

Q10

SELECT DISTINCT ?sensor WHERE {
      eau:eautonomeDeployment sosa:deployedSystem ?sensor .
      ?sensor a ?sensorClass .
      ?sensorClass rdfs:subClassOf* sosa:Sensor .
    }
    ORDER BY ?sensor

Result: 14 sensors.

Q12

SELECT DISTINCT ?mp WHERE {
      ?sensor eau:monitors ?mp ;
              eau:hasOperationalStatus ?status .
      FILTER(?status IN ("inactive", "planned"))
    }
    ORDER BY ?mp

Result: 4 measurement points.
